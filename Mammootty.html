Biography

Muhammad Kutty Panaparambil Ismail, professionally known as Mammootty, born on September 7, 1951, in Chandiroor, Kerala, India, is a legendary Indian actor and film producer who has predominantly worked in Malayalam cinema. With a career spanning over four decades, he has appeared in more than 400 films and is celebrated for his versatility, charisma, and powerful performances across genres.
